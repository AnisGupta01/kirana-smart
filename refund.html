Free demo first

You see a free demo in your shop's name before you pay anything. Please check it carefully on your own phone.

Setup fee (₹999)

  • Full refund if we cannot make your website live within 3 working days of receiving your payment and your shop details.
  • Full refund if you cancel before we start building your final website.
  • After your website is live, the setup fee is not refundable, because the work has been done. We will still fix anything that does not work as shown in the demo.

Monthly plans (₹149, ₹99, ₹499)

  • The first month of the Starter plan is free.
  • Stop anytime on WhatsApp. You will not be charged for the next month. The current paid month is not refunded part-way, and your website stays live until it ends.
  • If you were charged by mistake (for example, twice), we refund the extra amount in full.

Festival posters (₹199) and template licences

Poster sets are refundable only if we have not yet sent the first design. Template licences are digital files and are not refundable once shared, except if the files do not work as described.

How refunds are paid

Ask on WhatsApp. Approved refunds are sent back to the same UPI ID within 7 working days.

Orders from customers

Refunds or returns for things customers buy from a shop are between the customer and that shop. Kirana Smart does not handle customer payments.
