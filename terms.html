The service

KiranaYap sets up a website for your shop where customers can see your items and send orders to your WhatsApp. Plans and prices are shown on the pricing section of the home page. Current prices: Starter ₹999 one-time setup plus ₹149 per month (first month free); Premium website ₹2,999 one-time setup plus ₹199 per month; Udhaar Khata Pro ₹99 per month; festival poster set ₹199. Template licence ₹1,499 to ₹2,999 and reseller plan ₹499 per month for freelancers and agencies (see For freelancers & agencies).
