Who we are

Kirana Smart ("we") builds simple websites for local shops in India. Shops ("you") use their website to receive customer orders on WhatsApp. This policy explains what personal data is involved and how it is handled, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What this website collects

  • No accounts, no tracking. This website has no login, no advertising trackers and no analytics scripts. It does not use cookies.
  • Saved on your own device only. Your cart, language choice, the demo dashboard and the Udhaar Khata demo are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. We cannot see this data. Clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • WhatsApp messages. When you tap a WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens with a message typed in. Nothing is sent until you press Send. What you send is then handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy, and by the person or shop you send it to.

When you contact us for a website

If you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your name, number and the shop details you share (shop name, items, prices, address). We use them only to make your demo and website and to talk to you about it. We do not sell or share them with anyone, except service providers needed to run the website (for example the free hosting service). You can ask us to correct or delete your data at any time on WhatsApp.

Shops are responsible for their customers' data

When a customer orders from a shop's website, the order (name, phone, address, items) goes from the customer's WhatsApp to the shop's WhatsApp. The shop decides how it uses that data and is responsible for it as the "data fiduciary" under the DPDP Act. Shops should use customer details only to fulfil orders and keep them safe.

Udhaar Khata

The Udhaar Khata is a record book only. Kirana Smart does not lend money, give credit, or collect payments. In the demo, entries stay in the browser on the device where they were written and are not backed up.

Children

This service is meant for shop owners and their adult customers. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights and grievances

You can ask to access, correct or erase the data you shared with us, and raise a grievance, by messaging us on WhatsApp. We aim to reply within 7 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
