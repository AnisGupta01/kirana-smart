Short, honest answers. Not here? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How fast will my website be live?

Within 1–2 working days after we receive your details: shop name, WhatsApp number, product list and the setup payment. You see a free demo in your shop's name first.

Is there a commission on orders?

No. Zero commission on every order. You pay only the one-time setup (₹999 Starter, ₹2,999 Premium) and the monthly fee (₹149 Starter, ₹199 Premium).

How do orders reach me?

The customer adds items to the cart, fills in name and delivery address (phone is optional) and taps "Order on WhatsApp". WhatsApp opens with a ready message listing every item, quantity, line total, the order total, the name and the address. When the customer presses Send, the order arrives on your WhatsApp. You reply to confirm.

How do customers pay?

Directly to you: cash on delivery or pickup, or to your own UPI. There is no online payment on the website, and money never passes through KiranaYap.

Does stock or price update automatically?

No. The website is static. Each product has an "available" flag and a stock number in your shop's file. When something runs out or a price changes: Stock/price changes WhatsApp par bhejiye — 24 ghante ke andar update (Mon–Sat). Up to 10 changes a month on Starter, 25 on Premium. Live stock sync needs a backend; see below.

Is there an owner dashboard or login?

Not a real one. The "Shop setup tool" on this site is a local demo: changes stay only in the browser you use, and do not reach customers. It can export a shop file that we then publish. A real login with an order list needs a backend (for example a free Supabase or Firebase project) and is a possible future add-on.

What works without a backend, and what does not?

Works today: your shop page with products, prices, photos, categories, search, cart, quantity controls, stock flags, WhatsApp order message, Hindi/English, QR code and posters, offline loading after the first visit. Needs a backend (not included): saving orders in a dashboard, live stock sync, owner login, online payments, delivery tracking.

Can I get my own domain (.in / .com)?

Yes. Your website first goes live on a free github.io link. If you want your own domain, you buy it (usually ₹500–1,000 a year) and we connect it.

What if a customer does not have WhatsApp?

On a phone without WhatsApp, or on a computer, the link opens WhatsApp Web or asks to install WhatsApp. Customers can also simply call you; your phone number can be shown on the site.

Are the demo shops real?

No. Sharma Kirana Store, Shree Mithai Ghar, Fresh Oven Bakery, Ghar Ka Swaad Kitchen and Sharma Chai & Mithai are fictional demo shops with sample products and prices. Orders from demos go to our number, so you can see exactly what a shop receives.

How do I cancel?

Tell us on WhatsApp. There is no lock-in. Your website stays online until the end of the month you paid for. The setup fee is not refundable once the website is live; see the Refund policy.

Do I need GST or FSSAI?

The website does not change your licences. Your shop's GST, FSSAI and other registrations remain your responsibility, the same as in your shop today.

Is the Udhaar Khata a loan service?

No. It is only a digital record book, like a paper khata. We never lend money or collect payments. In the demo, data stays on your own phone.
