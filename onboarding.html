How onboarding works

सार (हिंदी में)

  • WhatsApp पर "DEMO" भेजें: दुकान का नाम, शहर, क्या बेचते हैं।
  • 1 कामकाजी दिन में आपके नाम से फ़्री डेमो।
  • प्लान चुनें, सामान की लिस्ट भेजें, डेमो पसंद आने पर ही UPI से सेटअप फ़ीस।
  • लाइव: अपना लिंक, QR कोड, A4 पोस्टर और काउंटर कार्ड। स्टॉक/दाम के बदलाव WhatsApp पर भेजिए — 24 घंटे के अंदर अपडेट (सोम–शनि)।

Everything happens on WhatsApp. No visit, no app to install, no meeting.

  1. 1. Say hello (day 0)

    Send "DEMO" on WhatsApp with your shop's name, city and what you sell. Open WhatsApp

  2. 2. Free demo in your name (within 1 working day)

    We send a demo link with your shop's name and a few of your items. Open it on your own phone and place a test order to yourself.

  3. 3. Choose a plan and send your list

    Pick Starter (₹999 setup + ₹149/month) or Premium (₹2,999 setup + ₹199/month). Send your product names, prices, pack sizes and photos (or we use clean stock-style photos), delivery area, delivery fee and the WhatsApp number that should receive orders.

  4. 4. Check and pay setup by UPI

    We build the full website and send you the private preview link. When you are happy, pay the setup fee by UPI. No payment is taken before you approve.

  5. 5. Go live (within 1–2 working days after we receive your details)

    Your shop gets its own link (for example …/store/index.html?shop=your-shop), a QR code PNG, an A4 poster and a counter card. Share the link in WhatsApp groups and status, and stick the poster at the counter.

  6. 6. Every month

    Pay the monthly fee by UPI in advance. Send price and item changes on WhatsApp (10 a month on Starter, 25 on Premium). Stop anytime; the site stays up until the end of the paid month.

Checklist: what to keep ready

  • Shop name as on your board, and a photo of the board or logo (optional).
  • WhatsApp number for orders, shop timings and address.
  • Product list: name (Hindi name optional), price, MRP if different, pack size, category.
  • Delivery terms: area, fee, free delivery above ₹…, or pickup only.

What you get, and what you don't

  • You get: a fast shop website with search, categories, cart, stock flags and WhatsApp checkout, in Hindi and English, plus QR and posters.
  • Not included: online payments, an order dashboard, owner login, live stock sync, delivery staff. Orders are WhatsApp messages that you manage in WhatsApp.
