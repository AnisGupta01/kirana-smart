Questions

Sawaal-jawaab (FAQ)

Computer nahi aata. Can I still use it?

Yes. We set up everything. Orders come on your WhatsApp, which you already use. To change a price or add an item, just send it to us on WhatsApp.

Is there a commission on orders?

No. Zero commission, on every order. You pay only the one-time setup and the monthly fee of your plan.

Order kaise aayega? How will I get orders?

The customer picks items on your website and taps "Order on WhatsApp". A neat list with items, quantities, total, name and address opens in WhatsApp and comes to your number when the customer presses Send. You reply to confirm.

How do customers pay?

Directly to you: cash, or your own UPI. There is no online payment on the website and money never goes through us.

Does stock update by itself?

Not automatically. The website is a fast static site. Stock/price changes WhatsApp par bhejiye — 24 ghante ke andar update (Mon–Sat). Your plan covers up to 10 changes a month on Starter and 25 on Premium. Live stock sync needs a paid backend and is not part of these plans.

Can I get my own domain (.in / .com)?

Yes. Your website goes live on a free link first. If you want your own domain, you buy it (usually ₹500–1,000 a year) and we connect it for you.
