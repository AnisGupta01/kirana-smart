Questions

Sawaal-jawaab (FAQ)

Computer nahi aata. Can I still use it?

Yes. We set up everything. Orders come on your WhatsApp, which you already use. To change a price or add an item, just send it to us on WhatsApp.

Is there a commission on orders?

No. Zero commission, on every order. You pay only ₹999 once for setup and ₹149 a month, and the first month is free.

Order kaise aayega? How will I get orders?

The customer picks items on your website and taps "Order on WhatsApp". A neat list with items, total, name and address opens in WhatsApp and comes to your number. You reply to confirm.

How do customers pay?

Directly to you: cash, or your own UPI. Money never goes through us. You pay us only the setup and monthly fee, by UPI.

Can I get my own domain (.in / .com)?

Yes. Your website goes live on a free link first. If you want your own domain, you buy it (usually ₹500–1,000 a year) and we connect it for you.

Is the Udhaar Khata a loan service?

No. It is only a digital record book, like your paper khata. We never lend money or collect payments. In the demo, data stays on your own phone.

Do I need GST or FSSAI for this?

The website does not change your licences. Your shop's GST, FSSAI and other registrations remain your own responsibility, the same as in your shop today.
