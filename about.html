Kirana Smart makes simple, fast websites for local shops — kirana stores, sweet shops, bakeries and home or cloud kitchens — so their customers can see products and prices online and send orders straight to the shop's WhatsApp.

Who we are

Kirana Smart is a small, independent service based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, founded and run by Anish Gupta, a web developer. We work with shops over WhatsApp, so no office visit or meeting is needed.

What we build

  • A website in your shop's name and colours, with your products, prices and photos.
  • Search, categories, a cart and a checkout that sends a neat order list to your WhatsApp.
  • Hindi and English on every page.
  • A QR code, an A4 poster and a counter card so walk-in customers can order again from home.

What we promise

  • No commission. You pay a one-time setup and a small monthly fee. Every order and every rupee is yours.
  • No payments through us. Customers pay you directly, in cash or to your own UPI.
  • Honest demos. Every demo shop on this site is fictional and labelled "Demo", with sample products and prices.
  • No fake reviews. We are new, so you will not see testimonials, ratings, client logos or "1000+ shops" numbers here. We will only ever show real ones, with the shop's permission.
  • Clear limits. These are static websites: orders arrive on WhatsApp, not in an app. Live stock sync, owner logins and online payments need a paid backend and are not part of the current plans (see the FAQ).

Why a static website?

Your website is a set of light pages hosted on GitHub Pages. That keeps it fast on budget Android phones, private (no tracking, no customer database) and cheap to run, which is why the monthly fee can stay small.
